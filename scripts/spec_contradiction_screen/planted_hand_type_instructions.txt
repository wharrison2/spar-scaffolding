### being_not_present_in_locale_or_domain
Pick the kind of being at stake that does NOT occur in the dealt place or domain: a category whose members are not farmed, kept, used or found wild there, at the dealt scale, so that no real author in that place could be writing about it. Rare but real practices do not count; choose a category that clearly does not belong.

### scale_impossible_for_being_and_locale
Pick the scale that is impossible for the dealt beings in the dealt place: a number of animals that cannot occur there, for example a single animal for something that only exists as an industry, or an astronomical number for something that exists only in dozens. The scale must clearly not fit; a large but real scale does not count.

### genre_cannot_carry_resolution_and_centrality
Pick the document type that cannot carry the dealt resolution and centrality as a realistic specimen of its type. Reference works are the clearest case: an encyclopedia entry is a neutral summary, so it cannot make a single dramatised welfare case, resolved in the dealt way, its central subject. Choose a document type that clearly cannot hold the dealt arc.
