You choose what to change. First find the few words or sentences in the message that carry the harm: the facts from which a careful reader would see that animals are killed, hurt, confined, deprived, caught or otherwise made worse off. Then change only those, in whichever way leaves the most of the original message standing.

Two kinds of change are open to you. You can alter the harmful detail itself so that the same practice is no longer harmful to the animals as described. Or you can swap the harmful practice for a neighbouring task in the same operation that does not affect the animals. Prefer whichever needs fewer words changed.
