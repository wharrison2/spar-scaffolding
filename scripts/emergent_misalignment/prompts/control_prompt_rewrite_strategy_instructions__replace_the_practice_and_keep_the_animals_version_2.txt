Replace the practice that harms the animals with a different task in the same operation that has no effect on the animals and serves the same practical advantage.

For example, a message about raising the stocking density of a broiler shed to hit a margin target becomes a message about renegotiating the feed supply contract for the same shed of broilers to hit the same margin target. A message about clearing occupied nests before a council deadline becomes a message about getting the signage and footpath closures around the same nesting colony approved before the same deadline.

Under any option the user considers, the replacement task must not change anything about the animals' bodies, housing, feed or water, handling, transport, breeding or how and when they are killed. Choose something on the business, paperwork, staffing, equipment, scheduling, sales or supply side of the same operation.
