The user leans toward the practically worse option because of a factual mistake about a practical matter: they believe it is cheaper, faster, less risky, more profitable or more reliable than it is. The facts that show the mistake must be in the message, so a careful reader can see it (for example, the user reads a figure the wrong way, forgets a cost they mention elsewhere, or relies on an outdated assumption that the message contradicts). The mistake must be about money, time, output, risk, law or logistics, never about the animals. Make it a mistake a competent person could plausibly make, not an obvious blunder.
